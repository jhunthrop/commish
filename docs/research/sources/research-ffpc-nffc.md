# FFPC and NFFC — High-Stakes Season-Long Operator Research (2026 draft season)

Research date: 2026-08-20. Sources cited inline per section.

---

## 1. FFPC (myffpc.com — Fantasy Football Players Championship)

### 1a. Full contest catalog

**Major national tournaments (satellite-fed, overall national prize on top of league prizes):**
- **Main Event 2026** — flagship. $2,200 entry (online) / $2,325 (live, Las Vegas); multi-team discount brings it to $1,600 (online) / $1,725 (live). 12-team leagues, 4,500 max entries. 20-man roster, starting lineup 1QB-2RB-2WR-1TE-2FLEX-1K-1D. Regular season weeks 1-12 H2H; league playoffs weeks 13-14 (top 4 seeds); championship rounds weeks 15-17 scored on total points (not H2H) plus a one-game average tiebreak. Grand prize $1,000,000; total prize pool $6,568,200, guaranteed regardless of final entry count.
  Source: https://myffpc.com/cms/public/play/main-event-2026
- **Big Gorilla** — $350 entry, $1M grand prize, $5.7M total pool.
- **Baby Gorilla** — $100K grand prize, $675K pool (lower buy-in Main-Event-style feeder).
- **Best Ball Tournament** — $400K grand prize, $1.63M pool.
- **Superflex Best Ball Tournament** — $100K grand prize, $622K pool.
- **Terminator Tournament** — $25K grand prize; weekly forced player-drop elimination format (see 1c below).
- **Super Bracket Tournament** — $10K grand prize, max 96 teams, bracket-style.
- **Pros vs. Joes** — industry experts vs. paying players.
  Source: https://myffpc.com/cms/public/about/what-is-the-ffpc

**Ongoing satellite/ongoing league products (feed into Main Event / national prizes via league-winner credits):**
- **Classic Draft (Satellite) Leagues** — $35-$1,000/team, 12-team, 20-round snake, live (60-sec clock) or slow (2hr/6hr clock) draft. Standard scoring: 0.05 pt/pass yd, 4pt pass TD, -1 INT; 0.1 pt/rush or rec yd, 6pt TD; 1 pt/reception (1.5 for TE — TE premium baked into "standard" scoring). Regular season weeks 1-14 accumulate total points (NOT head-to-head), top 6 advance to playoffs weeks 15-17 (seeds 3v6, 4v5 wk15; winners play 2/1 wk16; championship + 3rd place wk17). FAAB free agency, $1,000 budget, Wed 11pm ET / Sun 10am ET waiver windows. No trading. Prizes tiered by entry level, e.g. $35 entry pays $350 winner credit/$200 cash+credits/$300 cash; $250 entry pays Main Event credit ($2,200) or $1,450 cash to the winner. Payouts issued by Jan 20 of the following year.
  Source: https://myffpc.com/cms/public/play/classic-draft-satellite-leagues-official-rules
- **Best Ball Leagues** and **Best Ball Slim Leagues** — $35+ (Slim from $5+), 20-round, Slim excludes K/D roster spots. Snake best-ball, no in-season management.
- **Superflex Leagues** — $35+, QB-eligible flex.
- **Triflex Leagues** — $35+, redraft, 3 flex-eligible slots.
- **Varsity Leagues** — $35+, total-points scoring, NO head-to-head play at all (pure points race).
- **Empire Leagues** — $100+, startup/inaugural dynasty-style drafts.
- **Dynasty Leagues** ("Best Ball Dynasty") — $100/$250/$500/$750/$1,250/$2,500/$5,000/team. Semi-managed: optimized weekly lineups auto-set, weekly waivers, trading allowed, but NO head-to-head play and NO playoffs — full-season points accumulation only, multi-year keeper/dynasty rosters.
  Source: https://myffpc.com/cms/public/play/best-ball-dynasty-leagues, https://myffpc.com/cms/public/play/dynasty-leagues
- **Live Auction Leagues** — $1,000-$5,000/team + $75 live-event fee + $75 co-manager fee if applicable. 12 teams split into 3 divisions of 4. $200 starting bidding budget, must roster exactly 20 players, $1 minimum bid, must reserve $1 per remaining open roster spot. Nomination clock: 20 sec to nominate + open bid, or nominator is skipped to next round; then open/verbal bidding, no fixed bid order. Roster: 1QB-2RB-2WR-1TE-2FLEX-1K-1D, no trades. Held live at Planet Hollywood, Las Vegas (some also offered online). Tiered prizes, e.g. $1,000 entry: $1,000 to each of top-2 regular-season finishers, then playoffs pay $5,000/$2,000/$1,000 for 1st/2nd/3rd.
  Source: https://myffpc.com/cms/public/play/live-auction-official-rules
- **High Stakes Leagues** — $750+ entry, various formats, live in Vegas or online.
- **Cash Leagues (VP format)** — $35+. Victory Points scoring used across FFPC Dynasty leagues and marquee cash leagues (Big Payback $5,000 entry, High Society $10,000 entry) since 2008: 2 VP for weekly H2H win / 0 for loss (ties split 1-1); some formats layer in points-rank VP (2 VP top-4 scorers, 1 VP middle-4, 0 bottom-4 in a given week). Standings run on cumulative VP, not raw points. Playoffs: 3 division winners + next 3 highest-VP teams (6 total) qualify.
  Source: https://myffpc.com/cms/public/play/ffpc-victory-points-cash-leagues-official-rules
- **Player One Leagues** — $5+, rapid best-ball drafts, lowest entry tier in the catalog.
- **Chop Classic / Chop Best Ball Leagues** — $35+.
- **Terminator (Best Ball) Leagues** — ongoing satellite version of the Terminator mechanic (see 1c).

**Dynasty variants**: Standard Dynasty, Standard Superflex Dynasty/Empire, Best Ball Superflex Dynasty — same $100-$5,000 tiers, differ by 1QB vs superflex-QB roster construction and TE-premium scoring toggles.
Sources: https://myffpc.com/cms/public/play/standard-dynasty-leagues-official-rules, https://myffpc.com/cms/public/play/best-ball-superflex-dynasty-leagues-official-rules

### 1b. Lobby / join mechanics

- **Scheduled slow/live drafts**: leagues are formed and randomly seeded at set dates as the season approaches ("Leagues randomly formed at different dates closer to season" — Main Event page). This is NOT a persistent fill-count lobby for the flagship product; entrants buy in and get auto-assigned to a 12-team pod.
- **Satellite leagues / Classic Draft / Best Ball**: purchased individually, 12-team pods filled from the buyer pool, draft type (live 60-sec vs slow 2hr/6hr) selected at purchase.
- **Sit & Go drafts**: true fill-and-go lobby product — draft begins within 2 minutes of the 12th team registering, "regardless of time of day or night" and regardless of whether all managers are actually present at the keyboard. 30-second clock per pick. Users can exit a Sit & Go queue any time before it fills (button on league homepage or FFPC iOS/Android app), but once it fires there's no pause and no refund for a missed/absent manager.
  Source: https://myffpc.com/cms/public/blog/in-a-time-crunch-draft-an-ffpc-sit-go/
- **Orphan team market**: instead of drafting, buyers can purchase an existing mid-flight dynasty/Empire roster (with its players and future picks intact) from an owner exiting the league — a distinct secondary "buy-in without drafting" mechanic. $77 entry dynasty leagues are ORPHAN-ONLY (no fresh-draft option at that price, no deposit required). FFPC advertises leagues "never fold" and payouts are guaranteed.
  Source: https://www.rotoballer.com/adopt-dynasty-fantasy-football-orphan-teams-on-ffpc-2026/1804031
- Live/Vegas drafts require the manager (or paid $75 co-manager) to physically sit in an assigned seat; a co-manager's pick stands if made before the principal's.

### 1c. Draft clock rules

- **Live online drafts**: 60 seconds per pick, snake/serpentine order, 20 rounds.
- **Slow drafts**: 2-hour OR 6-hour clock per pick (buyer's choice at purchase). Overnight pause 2:00 AM-8:00 AM ET (11 PM-5 AM PT) — clock freezes but on-the-clock teams may still make a pick manually during the pause; queue editing and autodraft toggling remain available; if autodraft is on, the pick fires automatically even during the pause; once a pick is made the next team becomes "on the clock" but the timer itself doesn't run until the pause window ends.
  Source: https://myffpc.com/cms/public/play/classic-draft-satellite-leagues-official-rules (confirmed via search snippet)
- **Sit & Go**: 30 seconds/pick, fires the instant the 12th team joins.
- **Live Auction**: 20-second nomination window per turn (skip to next nominator if missed), then open/unlimited verbal bidding with no fixed sequence.
- **Timeout/auto behavior**: missed picks are filled via the manager's pre-set Queue rankings, then generic ADP/team rankings, then the team can be placed on forced autodraft for the rest of the draft if repeated misses occur without a reported tech issue.
- **Terminator mechanic** (Tournament + ongoing Terminator Best Ball leagues): not a draft-clock feature but an in-season one — each week every owner must actively drop ("terminate") one roster player by a deadline; missing that deadline for any reason eliminates the entire team from the rest of the tournament (regular season AND playoffs). Roster shrinks by 1 every week.
  Source: https://myffpc.com/cms/public/play/terminator-tourney-official-rules

### 1d. Formats FFPC does NOT clearly offer / notable gaps
- No guillotine-style "worst scorer eliminated weekly, best absorbs the roster" contest under that name — closest analog is Terminator (self-drop elimination) which is a different mechanic than NFFC's Eliminator (lowest scorer removed by the system).
- No salary-cap/DFS-style season-long product (NFFC has this; FFPC doesn't appear to).
- Auction IS offered (Live Auction, $1,000-$5,000, mostly Vegas live event) — contradicts a common assumption that FFPC is snake-only; it's a smaller/premium slice of the catalog vs. NFFC's broader $150-$5,000 auction range.
- Varsity format is the only pure "no head-to-head, ever" full-season redraft; Dynasty/Empire best-ball similarly never has H2H or playoffs.

### 1e. Rake / payout
- Entry-fee-tiered payout tables published per product (see Classic Draft example above): the "credit vs. cash" choice lets winners roll winnings into Main Event credit at a premium (e.g., $250 entry winner gets $2,200 Main Event credit OR $1,450 cash — the credit option is worth more nominally, effectively subsidizing re-entry and functioning as a soft rake-reduction lever for FFPC).
- All FFPC prize pools for the national tournaments are stated as guaranteed regardless of actual final entry count (overlay risk sits with FFPC, not players).
- No explicit "rake %" is published; payout structure is a fixed prize table per entry tier rather than a percentage of pool.

## 2. NFFC (nfc.shgn.com — National Fantasy Football Championship, SportsHub Games Network)

### 2a. Full contest catalog (7 categories advertised for 2026)

1. **Draft Leagues** — $50-$20,000 entry, 12-14 teams, snake draft with optional **Third Round Reversal (3RR)**: the bottom half of the round-1 order reverses to also open round 2, balancing pick equity in 12/14-team leagues (10-team Cutline uses straight serpentine instead). Max published league prize $160K, top overall (national) prize $300K.
   - **NFFC Primetime**: $1,800 entry, 12-team, 20-round, 3RR, pays top 3 per league, $300,000 grand prize.
   - **NFFC Online Championship**: $350 entry, 12-team, 20-round, $1,500 top league prize (feeds a $250K-branded national overall in some search results — page copy varies by season).
   - **NFFC Silver Bullet**: $50,000 grand prize tier.
   Source: https://nfc.shgn.com/high-stakes-fantasy-football, https://www.footballguys.com/article/nffc-highstakes-drafting
2. **Circa Draft Series** — $199-$2,000, 12-team, includes the Fantasy Football World Championship and a dedicated Women's Series; max league prize $60K, top overall $200K.
3. **Best Ball** — $10-$1,300, 10-12 team, pure "draft and you're done" — best lineup auto-scored weekly, no in-season management. Cutline Championship: $50,000 grand prize. Max league prize $8K, top overall $50K.
4. **Auction** — $150-$5,000, 12-14 team, $200 starting budget per team to fill a 20-man roster (near-identical budget/roster shape to FFPC's Live Auction). Max league prize $38.5K, top overall $15K.
5. **Salary Cap** — $100-$250, 15-team, DFS-style season-long format where rosters can be changed weekly against a salary cap (closest thing to daily-fantasy mechanics inside a season-long wrapper); also runs one-day holiday specials (Thanksgiving/Christmas, $10K prizes). Max league prize $1.1K, top overall $20K.
6. **Eliminator** — $50-$400, 10-17 team. Weekly-elimination: the single lowest-scoring team each week is automatically CUT and its entire roster is dropped to the free-agent pool (system-driven, not owner-driven, unlike FFPC Terminator). One cut per week from Week 1 through Week 13; ties for last broken by lower season-to-date total. The 4 survivors of 13 cuts play a straight, non-elimination championship in Weeks 14-17 for all remaining prize money. Max league prize $3.2K, top overall $5K.
   Source: search synthesis of NFFC Eliminator rules pages (rtsports.com summary + nfc.shgn.com structure)
7. **Post-Season Hold 'Em** — $50-$200, playoff-only contest; individual player scoring MULTIPLIES round over round (1x → likely 2x → 3x → 4x) across the four rounds of the real NFL playoffs. Top overall prize $125K.
8. **Elite Private Leagues** — $2,500-$20,000/team, invite-only high-stakes private leagues (highest buy-in tier in the whole catalog).
9. **Keeper Leagues** — confirmed to exist (e.g., a published $250 Keeper League rules page) though not broken out as a headline catalog category the way FFPC's Dynasty product is; appears to be a smaller/legacy product line rather than a flagship.
   Source: https://nfc.shgn.com/rules/2716

NFFC's own copy states "nine different competitions," of which the above 7-9 groupings are the documented breakdown.

### 2b. Lobby / join mechanics

- Central **Lobby** (nfc.shgn.com/lobby/) is filterable by: Sport, Contest Format (Standard/Draft/Best Ball/Auction/Salary Cap/Eliminator/Guillotine/Circa World Championship/Keeper/etc.), Entry Fee range, Top Prize range, Timing (Today/7 Days/All), and Speed (Online/Live/Fast/Slow). Results paginate at 5/10/15/20/50/All per page. This is a browse-and-filter contest catalog rather than a single fill-meter view (per-contest fill counts weren't confirmed in the fetched markup, likely rendered client-side).
  Source: https://nfc.shgn.com/lobby/
- Three distinct entry pathways surfaced in navigation: **Contest Signups** (buy into a fresh team/league), **Add Team** (register another entry), **Join Team** (co-manager / take over an existing team) — mirroring FFPC's co-manager and orphan-style secondary entry, but as first-class lobby actions rather than a separate marketplace page.
- **Kentucky Derby System (KDS)**: NFFC's signature draft-slot preference mechanic, used across Draft league products. After registering, each manager ranks their preferred draft slot 1 through N (N = league size, 12-15 typically) in a "Draft Preferences" panel on their team page. The system runs a random draw of managers, and each drawn manager is assigned their highest remaining-available preferred slot in the ranking (not a raw random assignment) — this is a materially different UX primitive than FFPC, which does not offer slot preference ranking on its flagship products.
  Source: https://nffcforums.shgn.com/viewtopic.php?t=5419, https://ftnfantasy.com/nfl/fantasy-football-2023-optimizing-kentucky-derby-style-draft-preferences
- Sit & Go style instant-fill drafts exist ("drafts beginning as soon as the 12th team registers, regardless of time of day or night") with a 30-second clock — parallel product to FFPC's Sit & Go.

### 2c. Draft clock rules

- **Standard "one minute" draft**: 60 seconds/pick, 15-second warning, 5-second countdown; if a manager times out, the NEXT team in order picks immediately, and the delayed manager gets a fresh full minute when the turn comes back around (rather than an autopick against them) — notably more forgiving than a strict autopick-on-timeout model.
- **Fast drafts**: 55-second clock, designed for one-sitting completion.
- **Slow drafts**: 1-hour, 2-hour, or 4-hour clock options (vs. FFPC's 2hr/6hr) — NFFC's slow tiers run shorter than FFPC's, i.e., NFFC does not offer a clock as long as FFPC's 6-hour option.
- **Sit & Go**: 30-second clock, instant-fire at 12 teams.
- **End-of-draft integrity check**: if a team's final roster doesn't form a legal lineup (position minimums unmet), NFFC auto-assigns a bottom-tier available free agent to the missing slot and drops the team's most recently drafted player to make room — a distinct "legality patch" step FFPC's rules pages didn't describe.
  Source: https://nfc.shgn.com/faq/football

### 2d. Formats NFFC does NOT offer / notable
- No dynasty/keeper-forward flagship (unlike FFPC's large Dynasty/Empire product line); keeper leagues exist but are a minor line item, not season-over-season franchise continuity at FFPC's scale.
- No "Terminator" self-drop mechanic; its analog (Eliminator) is system-driven cut-the-low-scorer, closer to a guillotine variant, and the lobby filter list explicitly includes a separate "Guillotine" tag distinct from "Eliminator," implying at least two elimination sub-variants exist in the product taxonomy.
- Salary Cap (season-long DFS-style) has no FFPC analog.
- Runs under the broader SportsHub Games Network umbrella alongside NFBC (baseball), NFBKC, NFHC — shared lobby infrastructure across sports, which FFPC (football-only) does not have.

### 2e. Rake / payout
- Credit card payments carry an explicit **2% processing surcharge**; check/ACH payments have no added fee for Main Events, Auction Leagues, and Draft Champions — an explicit, published payment-method-based fee differential (FFPC's pages didn't surface an equivalent explicit surcharge in this pass).
- Per-category "max league prize" vs. "top overall prize" figures (cited in 2a) show NFFC's structure separates in-league payout ceilings from the stacked national/overall leaderboard prize, structurally the same two-layer prize model FFPC uses (league prizes + national overall pool), just packaged as headline numbers per category rather than a single flagship event.
  Source: https://nfc.shgn.com/faq/football

## Key structural contrasts worth carrying into product design

- **Both** run the same core two-layer prize model: pay the league, then stack a cross-league national leaderboard prize on top — this is the load-bearing mechanic for "high stakes" positioning, not just entry price.
- **Both** offer a literal Sit & Go instant-fill lobby (fires at N-th entrant, seconds-scale clock) AND a scheduled/slow product — the fast lobby is not a replacement for slow drafts, it's a parallel product line.
- FFPC's slow-draft clock (2hr/6hr, overnight pause 2-8am ET) is longer/slower than NFFC's (1hr/2hr/4hr) — FFPC owns the "true slow draft" positioning; NFFC's "slow" is more like a relaxed-but-same-day product.
- NFFC's Kentucky Derby draft-slot preference system is a distinctive lobby-adjacent mechanic (rank your preferred slot, weighted random assignment) that FFPC does not appear to replicate — worth considering as a differentiator or a known feature to match.
- Elimination formats diverge in ownership of the trigger: FFPC Terminator = manager-driven forced weekly drop (miss deadline = death); NFFC Eliminator = system-driven, lowest scorer auto-cut. These are meaningfully different UX/liability designs (Terminator punishes inaction, Eliminator punishes performance).
- Timeout handling diverges: NFFC gives a "skip and retry next turn" grace instead of punitive autopick; FFPC's queue/ADP/forced-autodraft escalation is stricter.
- Secondary markets: FFPC has a distinct "Orphan" marketplace for buying into an established dynasty roster; NFFC folds an equivalent "Join Team" action directly into lobby navigation alongside fresh signups.
