Same discipline as before: two neutrals, one typeface, color only as meaning. What changed is where the energy comes from. A wire of real activity runs under the nav. The hero sits under a faint floodlight with yard lines you feel more than see. Proof becomes a scoreboard. Draft clocks tick in red, seats fill in green, and cards lift to meet your hand. Every moving thing on this page is something actually happening, which is the only kind of pizzazz that ages well.
