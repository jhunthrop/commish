# "Commish" as a name: trademark and store search, 2026-10-27

Internal. Prompted by Apple Developer Support: the team name "Commish LLC" is already in use by another developer, and Apple offered "Commish Limited Liability Company" instead (same legal entity; accepted). Searched the public web, the USPTO records as mirrored by Justia, the App Store and Google Play. Not legal advice; counsel's questions are at the end.

## What the USPTO treats the word as
- **"COMMISH" is descriptive in fantasy football.** COMMISH KIT (serial 77519625, Ed Frias, filed 2008, registered 2009 as 3704183 for "entertainment services in the nature of fantasy football leagues", class 41) carried a required **disclaimer of "COMMISH"**: the office would not let the applicant claim the word on its own for fantasy-league services. The registration was cancelled under Section 8 in 2016. Its sibling C COMMISH FANTASY FO CHEAT SHEETS (77519699, registered 3653572, cancelled 2016) disclaimed "COMMISH", "FANTASY" and "CHEAT SHEETS".
- Consequence both ways: nobody is likely to own the bare word for fantasy services, and neither are we. Protection, if any, comes from the whole mark (word plus the whistle mark, the way it is drawn) and from use.

## Live products using the name
1. **commish.live, "Commish"**: a full fantasy football platform (live in-game swapping, real-time waiver auctions, skill-based tournaments, a live trade market), iOS, Android and desktop, an FSGA member, marketed for 2026, screenshots reference commish.app. **This is the one that matters**: same word, same category, consumer-facing, live now. Its App Store and Play listings did not surface in search (the Play listing `com.commish.app` named "Commish" belongs to the unrelated affiliate app below), so its store name and launch date are unverified.
2. **"The Commish - Run Your League"** (App Store id6801585438, Henry Ventura, about August 2026): a commissioner companion (dues ledger, punishments, constitution, history). Adjacent, not a platform.
3. **"Commish App" / "Commish"** by Commish Technologies, LLC (App Store id6752741734, Play `com.commish.app`, getcommish.app): an affiliate-marketing app, unrelated category. Likely one of the holders behind the Apple team-name collision, together with the California and Florida "Commish LLC" registrations (unrelated businesses).
4. Commish Kit (cheat sheets and draft kits since 2002), The Commish Brand (apparel), TrophySmack's "Commish" chain: adjacent merchandise, not software.

## App Store and Play naming
- Apple requires an app name unique across the store. "Commish" alone may already be taken (by commish.live's app if it is listed under that name, which is unverified). Our listing name should be ready to carry a distinguishing suffix the way the companion app does ("Commish: Draft and Lineup", "Commish Fantasy Prep"). The subtitle and keyword fields carry the rest. Play allows duplicate titles but ranks the older one.
- The team name shown to buyers will read "Commish Limited Liability Company". A DBA display name is not something Apple lets an organization set itself; it can be requested through support later if it matters.

## Assessment
- **Trademark risk from others to us**: low to moderate. The word is descriptive in this field, the registered uses are apparel and a cancelled league-services mark, and the two pending or recent marks (CROOKED COMMISH, LEAGUE COMMISH) are composite marks in adjacent services. The live risk is commish.live: an unregistered but in-use mark in the same category predates our launch, so common-law priority could sit with them in their trading area. A conflict would most likely arrive as a store-name dispute or a demand letter, not a registration refusal.
- **Our ability to own the name**: weak for the bare word; a combined mark (word plus whistle) in classes 9 (software), 41 (fantasy sports information services) and 42 (online software) is filable but would be examined against commish.live's prior use if they file first or oppose.
- **What to do**: (1) keep the name for now; it is launch-free this season and the apps are not yet in review; (2) file an intent-to-use application for the combined mark in classes 9, 41 and 42 before the store submissions if the founder wants a priority date (about $350 per class in USPTO fees, no counsel required to file, counsel recommended); (3) prepare a store name with a suffix; (4) ask counsel the two questions below before the App Store submission.

## Questions for counsel
1. Does commish.live's in-use, unregistered "Commish" in the same category block or weaken a combined-mark application by Commish LLC, and what is the exposure if they send a demand after our store launch?
2. Is the Apple seller name "Commish Limited Liability Company" plus an app name with a suffix enough to avoid a likelihood-of-confusion claim, given the USPTO treats "commish" as descriptive for fantasy services?
