# FantasyPros DFS tools: catalogue, paywall, member voice, and the map to Commish

**Status:** internal research, requested 2026-10-26. Competitor names are fine here; never in product copy ([no-competitor-names]). Nothing here is a ruling; the founder's rules that govern it are research-only premium, no betting or trading words on the site, no gambling NAICS, and no weekly model claimed before the record shows it.
**Read date:** every page below was read on 2026-10-25 (the machine's date while this was written; the optimizer's expert list showed projections "updated 10/25"). "Not found" means I looked and could not confirm it; nothing is guessed.
**Limits:** FantasyPros' help centre (support.fantasypros.com) and draftkings.com/help/terms answered HTTP 403 to direct fetch; two help articles were read through a page-reader service and are marked **(reader)**, others only through search excerpts, marked **(excerpt)**. Reddit could not be reached (three searches returned no threads), and the archive.org snapshot of last year's plans page could not be fetched, so the year-over-year comparison leans on dated third-party reviews.

## Summary

FantasyPros' DFS suite is narrow and almost entirely behind its top tier: the lineup optimizer (up to 150 lineups, locks, excludes, exposure caps, a stack finder, GPP or cash mode, your own projections, CSV export), daily projections built from its expert consensus (the member can pick which of 100+ ranked sources to include), DFS cheat sheets and value plays (projected points against salary, with Vegas total and spread), projected roster percentages (shown for FanDuel; DraftKings not confirmed), salary changes week over week, a saved-lineups history, and a contest-history ROI analyzer fed by the member's own DraftKings or FanDuel CSV. Sites are DraftKings, FanDuel and Yahoo, with OwnersBox (and a FantasyDraft page) for the optimizer; sports are NFL, MLB and NBA. PRO ($3.99 a month billed yearly) and MVP ($5.99) carry no DFS tool; HOF ($8.99 a month billed yearly, $11.99 semi-annually, $22.99 monthly, 3-day trial for new customers) carries all of it, and the plans page also shows a $2.99 a month DFS module whose attachment rules it does not state. Pick'em (Underdog, PrizePicks) is not on FantasyPros at all: the sister brand BettingPros launched PrizePicks and Underdog optimizers on 2026-08-18 that grade picks against sportsbook odds. No late-swap tool, Sunday live DFS tool, simulation, or contest simulator was found. Member voice on the DFS side is thin: one 2025 review calls the optimizer alone worth HOF at a fraction of $99-a-month rivals, and its one DFS complaint is that projected ownership is missing for some sites; listicle rankings place it well behind simulation tools. For Commish, the hard ingredient is already in the store: Tank01's `getNFLDFS` capture carries DraftKings, FanDuel and Yahoo salaries per date. What we lack is ownership data, slate definitions, a weekly number of our own (rounds 2, 3, 4 and the K/DEF sub-round failed their checks), and clear rights to display salaries. A research-only DFS shelf (salary beside the published projection, salary movement, matchup, weather and game facts, teammate correlations from history, and a public grade of how points per salary actually landed) fits the founder's rules; an optimizer with export, an ROI and profit analyzer, ownership "leverage" and anything pick'em do not fit, or need a fresh ruling and counsel first.

## 3. How the tools work

- **Projections:** consensus of expert sources, "weekly, for NFL" ([help (reader)](https://support.fantasypros.com/hc/en-us/articles/115001366668-How-does-the-DFS-Lineup-Optimizer-work)). Member can select sources from 100+ listed with 2026 in-season accuracy ranks (Yahoo Sports, Draft Sharks, PFF, FTN, Fantasy Life, Establish the Run, The Action Network and others) ([optimizer](https://www.fantasypros.com/daily-fantasy/nfl/lineup-optimizer.php?try=true)). No model of their own is described for DFS; no simulation, no distribution beyond "GPP settings ... to utilize boom/bust players" (help, 2020).
- **Optimizer inputs the member sets:** site and slate; player locks, includes, excludes; team or game filters; stack picks (include or not include players from a team); number of lineups (to 150); max share of the cap; exposure caps; GPP or cash; FLEX and TE-in-FLEX rules; own projections. **Output:** an optimal lineup, then a set of lineups sortable by projected points or salary, saved and exported as CSV. An error modal lists infeasible settings.
- **Cheat sheet and value plays:** projected points, projected rank against salary rank, cost per point, Vegas spread and total; value updated "daily with hourly adjustments".
- **Ownership:** projected roster percentage per player on FanDuel; how it is made is not stated on any page read.
- **ROI analyzer:** member's own contest-history CSV in, profitability by contest type out.
- **Game day cadence:** only "hourly adjustments" (value plays) and per-source update stamps were found; no stated Sunday-morning inactive refresh, late swap, or live tool.

## 4. What members say

The DFS suite gets little direct member discussion that I could reach; what follows is all of it, cited.

**Most praised**
1. **The optimizer as value for the money.** "the lineup optimizer alone makes the HOF package a no-brainer"; HOF gives "full access to their DFS interface for a fraction of what you'll pay for other daily fantasy tools"; "some DFS tools cost over $99/month for similar functionality" ([CheatSheetWarRoom, 2025-08-18](https://www.cheatsheetwarroom.com/blog/fantasy-football/site/fantasypros-review)).
2. **Custom projections and locks/excludes** are singled out in the same review.
3. **Consensus projections and simplicity** are its niche in listicle rankings: ninth of ten at 7.6 against SaberSim 9.8 and Stokastic 9.2 ([Zipdo, 2026](https://zipdo.co/best/daily-fantasy-sports-software/)); "a projection-first approach" fit for "faster slate-to-lineup iteration" ([Gitnux, 2026](https://gitnux.org/best/daily-fantasy-algorithm-software/)). These are auto-generated rankings; weigh lightly.

**Most common complaints**
1. **Ownership coverage:** "Daily fantasy projected ownership tool isn't available for some sites" (CheatSheetWarRoom con list, 2025-08-18). Consistent with the hub showing only a FanDuel ownership page.
2. **No simulation:** the 2026 comparison pages that rank DFS tools build on game or contest simulation (Stokastic, SaberSim) and either omit FantasyPros or rank it low ([Stokastic comparison](https://www.stokastic.com/articles/nfl-dfs/stokastic-sims-vs-sabersim-vs-rotogrinders-nfl-2026), [FantasySportsEdge, 2026-10-02](https://fantasysportsedge.com/best-dfs-lineup-optimizers)). This is an inference from the field, not a quoted member complaint.
3. **Price and paywall drift (general, not DFS-only):** monthly prices "can seem a bit inflated" (CheatSheetWarRoom); NFL DFS "now requires payment, no longer free" ([DFS101](https://www.dailyfantasysports101.com/tools/)); app reviewers call subscriptions "a little pricey" (App Store, 2019 featured review). Trustpilot is 2.5 on about 6 old reviews, none DFS ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.fantasypros.com)).

Gap: no Reddit or forum thread was reachable; the 2026-10-15 in-season study's 500 App Store reviews were not re-mined for DFS here.

## 5. The map to Commish

Ingredients we hold today: the rest-of-season board; the published weekly projection (provider's, shown labelled until a round of ours passes); game state per screen; matchup and weather facts; injuries, inactives, depth charts; Tank01 `getNFLDFS` captured on game days with **DraftKings, FanDuel and Yahoo salaries per date** (store source `tank01_dfs_salaries`, see `research/pipeline/tank01/client.py` and the 2026-10-12 parity fixture: 754 DraftKings, 741 FanDuel, 499 Yahoo rows); Tank01 betting odds and The Odds API player props in the store; nflverse play-by-play and weekly stats history; the research record. The 2026-09-07 fact-sheet inventory judged the DFS salary feed "low value or off-limits" for the peek, which is a ruling about that sheet, not about a DFS page.

Cross-cutting collisions:
- **Research only:** a DFS shelf is information a manager could get elsewhere, and it touches no Commish contest; it does not break [premium-is-research-only]. The line is crossed by export to an operator, auto-built multi-entry portfolios, or any "win" copy.
- **Gambling NAICS and affiliate money:** FantasyPros' DFS hub links a FanDuel affiliate lobby and the plans page sells sportsbook and prediction-market partner deals. Any affiliate revenue from DFS or sportsbook operators is the item to keep off Commish; it is the kind of revenue that could change how the LLC is classified (counsel question, not answered here).
- **Words:** DFS's native vocabulary (chalk, leverage, ROI, edge, Vegas, odds, value) collides with the betting and trading word bans in places; a guard-test word list for a DFS page would need extending.
