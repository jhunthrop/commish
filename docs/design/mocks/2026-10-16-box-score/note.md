# Lane 66d mock: the Matchup screen as a box score

Each team is a table with five columns: Slot, Player, Game, A game, Pts. The player is the biggest thing on the row: a 32 px face, his name, a rose "Questionable" chip beside the name, and club and position in small caps underneath. The Game cell has two lines. The first is "at JAX Sun 8:30 am". The second holds the expected score in the number face ("20 to 22.5", his club first) and a glyph with two or three words ("Abroad, no forecast", "Indoors", "Roof TBD", "74°, 15 mph"). The read time is gone from the rows and said once in the basis line. A game is right-aligned with one decimal and sized like a stat. The head is short, so it can turn into "Wk 6" when a weekly round passes. Kickers and defenses say "None", and the Totals row says it counts six of eight. Pts shows "--" until the game is played. The fun comes from the score bug, which appears twice. Across the top it puts both teams, the avatars and the first kickoff on the black scoreboard, with your side in orange. Each table's Totals row is the same board. Bench rows sit below in the same columns, shorter and quieter, with byes marked "Bye". At 390 the tables stack with yours first, and the Game cell folds under the name.

Data: names, slots and statuses as the coordinator gave them. Clubs and ESPN ids come from dev nfl_players (read only). Game facts come from the fixture's games.json. A game uses the fixture's Lane 64 figure where it has one (Irving, Burden, Kittle and the bench). The other starters' figures are stand-in preseason points a game (projected over expected games). Team avatars are initials because we hold no host avatar.
