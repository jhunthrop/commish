# The Commish mark: the whistle (2026-10-24)

The founder chose the whistle on 2026-10-24 from a generated sheet and produced the five reference images here with an image model; the coordinator traced the light master into `mark.svg` (one path and one disc in a 256 box) and `mark-small.svg` (the same path at 1.6 times the stroke, for 32 px and under). `trace-both-themes.png` shows the trace at 16, 32, 64, 128 and 256 in both themes, beside the name and on a tile.

Direction in three sentences. The mark is a referee's whistle reduced to one continuous outline: a round chamber, a barrel that runs flat along the top and tapers to a slanted tip, no ring, no notch. It carries exactly one accent, the pea, a solid disc in the theme's accent. Ink and accent come from the site's tokens (light: ink #15120f, accent #c2470a; dark: ink #f5f0ea, accent #ff7130), so the mark is the same drawing in both themes.

Surfaces: the favicon, the installable app's icons (192, 512, maskable, apple touch), the rail and phone-bar mark in the shell, the share cards. One module holds the path; every surface draws from it.

The old league-platform mark (`src/app/icon.svg`, the whistle on a navy tile with the amber pea) is the previous product's and stays untouched there.
