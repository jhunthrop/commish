System

Rules

  1. The person before the numberEvery row opens on a face and a name. The number comes after.
  2. One big number that says what it isEach screen has one number at display size, and the words under it name its basis.
  3. One solid verb per viewportBurnt orange fills one button on screen. Everything else is quiet.
  4. Every colored cell names its numberGreen prints its round, rose prints its minus, a tier strip prints its drop.
  5. No explainer textIf a label needs a tip, change the label.
