Three directions for Commish

Each is a complete identity: a governing idea, a type pairing, a palette in light and dark, radii and materials, density rules, a motion vocabulary. All three honour the principles in the first-principles document. They disagree about what the product feels like to be inside. Every mock is a full screen at 1440 with the phone layout in the same file. Numbers are real where the repo holds them and labelled as stand-ins in source where it does not.
