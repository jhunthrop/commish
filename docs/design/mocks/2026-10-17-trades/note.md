# Lane 66g mock: the Trades screen as a trade table

The page now reads like a box score. At the top, one strip says "Two trades worth proposing". Each trade is a card that names the partner, the swap ("Rodgers for Skattebo and Strong"), both gains ("+5.7 a week for you, +0.7 for them"), the drop, and one verb, "Open Sleeper trades". Sleeper takes no proposals from here, so that verb copies the trade.

Below the strip is the finder, a table with one trade per row and the best trade with each team. Its columns are You give, You get, With, then two ruled-off groups, Rest of season a week (You, Them, sorted) and This week Proj (You, Them), then Room. One tap opens the row: every player in the trade in the Matchup columns (Proj, Pts, Opp rank, Avg, Last, Rest of season), with "Starts RB" on the player who would start.

The builder is two box scores with tick boxes under a black scoreboard. Each side shows its Proj and rest-of-season totals before and after, plus Room, with Propose in the middle. Lane 61's answer and its honesty sentence sit under the scoreboard. On a phone the cards stack and keep their own verbs, and an open builder pins its gain and Propose.
