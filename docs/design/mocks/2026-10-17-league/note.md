# Lane 66i mock: the League page as a standings and roster board

The week's matchups lead the page as six score bugs on the lit board, three across. Each bug shows the state, then both teams with their avatar, record, Proj and Pts. Yours comes first with an orange rim, and every bug opens its Matchup. Below that, Standings sits on the left as a box score: #, Team with its manager, W-L-T, PF, PA, a Streak chip, this week's Proj and FAAB left on Sleeper (waiver order on ESPN). Your row is tinted, an orange rule marks the playoff spots, and the headers sort. On the right, Your lineup uses the Lineup table's columns (Slot, Player, Game, Proj, Pts). Each starter the advice sits shows "Dart starts, 20.6" under his name. One verb sits above: "Set your lineup", with "Three changes, +26.9". On ESPN with the lineup set, it reads "Lineup set on ESPN" instead. Moves is a feed table: When, Team, Move, Players (in and out arrows) and the FAAB bid, with your moves tinted. Settings, behind the Week 6 / Settings toggle, lists all 42 rules in the setup page's groups. It shows the points-allowed and yards-allowed tiers as two ladders, what Sleeper scores that we leave out, the roster shape and the league terms, plus a link to Sleeper.

API gaps for the build: points against, streak, waiver budget or order, avatars, every pairing with each team's Proj and state, bids, ESPN lineup moves, and the playoff settings.
