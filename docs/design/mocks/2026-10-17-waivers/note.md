# Lane 66f mock: the Waivers screen as a pickup table

Waivers is the Lineup table turned outward. Above it, one strip: "Week 6, Two pickups", a card for each pickup with its slot, the move ("Add Gainwell, drop Lemon"), both gains ("+6.7 Proj this week, +1.2 a game rest of season") and its own verb, "Open ESPN waivers". That verb copies the name and opens ESPN's page, because claims are not sent to ESPN from here yet. With the claim switch on, it reads "Add on ESPN" and opens the confirm. The strip's 96.4 builds on the Lineup page's 87.0, so the two pages agree. A pickup makes the strip only if he starts this week and does not lower the rest of season. So Mevis (+0.2 over Fairbairn) stays in the table, and Allen, on bye, beats no one. The table keeps Lineup's columns, then the two only we give: Rest of season, and Beats (the player he tops, with the gain, green for a starter, grey for the bench). The default sort is Beats. Add opens the drop panel inside the table, in the same columns, with the advice's drop first. Each drop shows its season gain, and the week's gain is said once. On the phone, the cards scroll, the verb is pinned, and the first row clears it.

Data: the roster and its figures are Lane 66e's mock. The pool is a stand-in list of real players. Every Proj is the week 1 figure. Rest of season and Last are real where the fixture league holds the player.
