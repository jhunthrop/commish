**Goal:** Reshape the whole product so it reads as a fantasy site rather than a legal one. Founder, 2026-08-27: *"It feels like a legal website and not a fantasy site"* and *"I wanted to reshape the entire site to be fun like flock fantasy."*

**Spec:** `docs/design/2026-08-27-fun-pass-spec.md`. Read it before Task 1 — it carries the rule amendments, the colourblind rule, and the reasoning this plan only executes.

**Architecture:** Phase 1 builds the VOCABULARY (tokens + primitives) and ships no screen. Phases 2-5 apply it, surface by surface. ## Global Constraints

- **Three rules move** (spec §Amendments): rule 1 gains magnitude, tier grade and team identity; rule 4 gains the card test (*act on or compare = card; read down a column = row*); rule 5 keeps holding except on named action-dense surfaces.
- **Three rules hold:** one typeface at ≤600 weight, no caps, only true things move. Dynamic range comes from SIZE, never from heavier type.
- **Magnitude colour is never the only channel.** Green/red is the worst pair for deuteranopia and this audience is overwhelmingly men. Every heat-coloured value also reads plainly as its own number, and a judged value carries a weight step or glyph. A manager who sees no colour must still rank correctly. This is a hard gate, not a nicety.
- **Magnitude may never appear on a control.** That is what keeps "green fill = action" intact.
- `src/design/tokens.test.ts` (140 contrast assertions) stays green. New pigment clears its own threshold or does not ship.
- `e2e/page-audit.spec.ts` asserts at most one visible ink action per audited page. Do NOT add routes to it and do NOT weaken it.
- The testids `pool-player-name` / `pool-player-team` are read by `e2e/mock-draft.spec.ts` and `e2e/league-lifecycle.spec.ts`. Break them and those specs hang for their full budget — that happened on 2026-08-27.
- No em-dashes in user-facing copy. Persona review applies to every copy change.
- Never touch `/Users/jh/code/fantasy`'s working tree or any other worktree. Stage explicit paths.
