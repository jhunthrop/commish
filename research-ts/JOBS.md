# The job inventory: what the Vercel tick runs today, and where each job goes

Written 2026-10-12 at the research carve-out (Lane 2). The old app's only Vercel cron is `/api/jobs/tick` (`vercel.json`, every minute). The tick runs the Postgres-backed job system: the code-defined schedules in `src/jobs/schedules.ts` enqueue jobs, and code paths in the league app enqueue the on-demand ones; `src/jobs/registry.ts` registers ten definitions. Nothing else in the repository is scheduled by Vercel.

Destinations:

- **nightly**: part of the research chain, run by `src/nightly/main.ts` (`npm run nightly`), one pass a night.
- **in-season**: a research job that must keep its own cadence through the 2026 season because what it records depends on when it runs. Carried by `src/inseason/main.ts` (`npm run inseason -- --task=<task>`), one pass per invocation, the scheduler owns the cadence.
- **league-app**: dies with the Next.js app at cutover.

## Not on the tick, recorded so nothing is forgotten

- **The stats pipeline worker** (`src/stats-pipeline`, run by `scripts/ingest-once.ts` and `scripts/jobs-runner.ts`, never by the Vercel cron): the provider's live stat lines into `player_stat_lines`, `stat_weeks`, `nfl_games` and `players` for in-season league scoring. The capsule facts read `player_stat_lines` and `stat_weeks` for the current season's summaries, so if the 2026 season's live lines are to keep landing after Vercel is torn down, this worker needs a home: it is league-app code today and is **not** carried by either research entry point. The research core's own outcomes come from nflverse through `season-week`, which is.
- **The operator scripts** in `research-ts/scripts` (the fits, the backfills, the reports, `preseason-persist.ts`, `register-freeze.ts`, `register-grade.ts`) run by hand and are not scheduled.

## What the 2026 capture needs after Vercel is torn down

Exactly the two in-season tasks on their current cadences (`season-week` three times a week, `capture-harvest` hourly in waking hours plus 02:00 ET) and the nightly run. Everything else on the tick is league-app and stops with it.
